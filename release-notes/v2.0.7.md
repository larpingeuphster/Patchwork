## New: Cache tab

- **See everything Roblox has downloaded**, most recently used first: look at something in RIVALS, press **Refresh**, and it's at the top.
- **Previews:** images, KTX2 textures (decoded), and sounds you can play.
- **Filter** by textures, images, meshes, models, sounds, or what you've replaced.
- **Click a file** to:
  - **Replace with file…** your own image, texture or sound. Textures are converted to the same KTX2 format and size; sounds must be the same format (.ogg for .ogg).
  - **Put original back.**
  - **Save a copy** of the file.
- Replacements are kept like any other swap: through Clear Cache, restarts and Roblox downloading the file again.

This needs no asset IDs, so it works for RIVALS' own textures, sounds and skins, which Roblox only gives to signed-in players.
