**Patchwork 2.0 swaps assets in Roblox's cache**, the same way rbxcache does, so it works again after Roblox blocked network-based swappers like Fleasion.

> [!WARNING]
> **You can be banned from RIVALS for using skin changers.** Swapping weapon or character skins with Patchwork counts as one, even though only you can see it. Use Patchwork at your own risk, and try it on an alt account first.

## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v2.0.0-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 1.x, and your profiles and settings carry over.

## What changed

1.x tried to catch Roblox's asset downloads on the network. Roblox now pins its certificates, so it refuses any connection that isn't straight to its own servers. That blocked 1.x completely, and it's the same block Fleasion has hit.

2.0 works differently. Roblox saves every asset it downloads in a cache on your PC, and Patchwork swaps the cached copies:

1. **Finding each asset.** For each asset ID you swap, Patchwork downloads the original once from Roblox (signed out) to learn its fingerprint, then finds the matching entry in Roblox's cache.
2. **Swapping it.** It backs up the cached original and writes your replacement in its place, keeping Roblox's record of the download valid.
3. **Showing up.** Roblox uses your version the next time it loads that asset, usually your next match. If Roblox downloads it again, Patchwork swaps it again.
4. **Putting it back.** Turning a swap off, or quitting Patchwork, puts the original back.

That means:

- **No more network interception, certificates or Roblox setting changes.** 2.0 also removes the certificate setup that 1.x added.
- **It works with Roblox open, and with any launcher** (Roblox, Bloxstrap, Fishstrap, Froststrap).
- **No more blank icons or images in Roblox.**

## What can be swapped

- **Images** (textures, skyboxes, UI, item backgrounds): PNG, JPG and WEBP are converted to match the cached original. **Remove** makes them transparent.
- **Skins:** a mesh or model swapped for another skin's mesh or model, by asset ID.
- **Sounds:** only some, for now. Roblox only lets signed-in players download most sounds, and Patchwork never uses your account, so most sounds can't be found in the cache automatically yet. Activity tells you which.
- **Not allowed:** removing or blanking 3D objects (that would make them invisible), and `.obj` files.
- **Not yet:** textures Roblox stores as GPU texture packs (KTX2). Activity tells you when an asset is one of these.

## How to use it

1. Quit the old Patchwork from its tray icon, then open 2.0.0.
2. Play RIVALS as normal. Watch **Activity**: it lists each asset as it's swapped, and a summary of how many are swapped, waiting for Roblox to download, or can't be swapped and why.
3. A swap shows up the next time Roblox loads that asset, so if you don't see it straight away, play another match.
