Patchwork now lives in the system tray, like Fleasion.

> [!WARNING]
> **You can be banned from RIVALS for using skin changers.** Swapping weapon or character skins with Patchwork counts as one, even though only you can see it. Use Patchwork at your own risk, and try it on an alt account first.

## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v1.0.6-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 1.0.5, and your profiles and settings carry over.

## New

- **System tray.** Closing the window no longer quits Patchwork: it keeps running in the system tray (next to the clock), so your swaps keep working. Right-click the tray icon for:
  - **Open Patchwork**: double-clicking the icon does this too.
  - **Clear Roblox cache**: closes Roblox if needed and clears the cache, so the next time Roblox opens it loads your swaps.
  - **Quit Patchwork**: stops swapping and puts Roblox back to normal.
- **Starts in the tray with Windows.** When Patchwork starts at sign-in, it goes straight to the tray instead of opening a window.
- **Tray setting.** If you'd rather closing the window quit Patchwork, turn off **Settings → Keep running in the tray when I close the window**.
- **New purple icon** for the app and the tray.

## Every time you play

You don't need to do anything: Patchwork starts with Windows, sits in the tray, and your enabled profiles apply when you open RIVALS. If swaps ever don't show, right-click the tray icon → **Clear Roblox cache**, then open RIVALS again.
