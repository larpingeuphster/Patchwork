**Hotfix:** in 1.0.5 and 1.0.6, some Roblox images (icons, avatars, UI pictures) didn't load while Patchwork was running. This version fixes that.

> [!WARNING]
> **You can be banned from RIVALS for using skin changers.** Swapping weapon or character skins with Patchwork counts as one, even though only you can see it. Use Patchwork at your own risk, and try it on an alt account first.

## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v1.0.7-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 1.0.6, and your profiles and settings carry over.

## Fixed

- **Blank icons and images in Roblox.** 1.0.5 started routing Roblox's content download servers (`fts.rbxcdn.com` and `contentdelivery.roblox.com`) through Patchwork, and Roblox's downloads from them failed. Patchwork no longer touches those servers, so Roblox loads normally again. This also takes them out of settings saved by 1.0.5 and 1.0.6.

## After updating

1. Quit the old Patchwork from its tray icon.
2. Open 1.0.7.
3. Right-click its tray icon → **Clear Roblox cache**, then open RIVALS.
