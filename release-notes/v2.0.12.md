Find a skin's files without asset IDs: Watch in the Cache tab.

> [!WARNING]
> **You can be banned from RIVALS for using skin changers.** Swapping weapon or character skins with Patchwork counts as one, even though only you can see it. Use Patchwork at your own risk, and try it on an alt account first.

## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v2.0.12-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 2.0.11.

## New: Watch (Cache tab)

Press **Watch**, look at a skin in RIVALS, then press **Refresh**: the **Since watch** filter shows only the files Roblox downloaded or read for it, marked **New**, with 3D previews for meshes and pictures for textures.

## Fixed

- **Skin capture no longer clears the cache.** Clearing could hide the very skin being captured: Roblox keeps what it has loaded in memory and doesn't download it again, so nothing new appeared. Capture now records files Roblox downloads or reads while a step runs.

## Swapping a skin without asset IDs

1. Close Roblox completely, open RIVALS and wait in the lobby.
2. **Cache → Watch.** Open the preview of the skin you **want**. **Refresh.** Save a copy of its mesh and texture (click each → **Save a copy**).
3. **Watch** again (Stop watching, then Watch). Equip the skin you **have**. **Refresh.** Click its mesh → **Replace with file…** → pick the saved mesh; same for its texture.
4. Close Roblox completely and open RIVALS again to see it.

Look at each skin for the first time in that session: one Roblox already has in memory isn't loaded again.
