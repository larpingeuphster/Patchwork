Sounds and animations: use any audio file, and find animations in the Cache tab.

> [!WARNING]
> **You can be banned from RIVALS for using skin changers.** Swapping weapon or character skins with Patchwork counts as one, even though only you can see it. Use Patchwork at your own risk, and try it on an alt account first.

## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v2.0.14-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 2.0.13.

## Sounds

- **Replace a sound with any audio file:** OGG, MP3, WAV or FLAC. Patchwork converts it to the format Roblox stored the original in (usually OGG), both in the Cache tab and for preset sound swaps.
- Sounds show their **length**, sample rate and channels when you open them.

## Animations

- **Animations are recognised.** In Roblox's cache an animation is just a model file; Patchwork now reads what's inside, so animations get their own **Animations** filter, a running-figure icon, and their name and makeup when you open them (e.g. “Slash” · 1 KeyframeSequence, 12 Keyframe).
- Other models are labelled too: *Model with effects*, *Model with sounds*, *Model (mesh parts)*.
- Replace an animation with another animation file (**Save a copy** of one first, e.g. from another skin you caught with **Watch**).

## Finding a weapon's sounds and animations

1. Close Roblox completely, open RIVALS.
2. **Cache → Watch.** Use the weapon in RIVALS: equip it, shoot, reload, inspect.
3. **Refresh.** Under **Since watch**, use **Sounds** and **Animations** to see what it loaded; press play on sounds to find the right one.
4. Click one → **Replace with file…**. Then close Roblox completely and open RIVALS again.
