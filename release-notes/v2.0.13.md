Use .obj models (like the Frostbite Bow pack's) to replace meshes.

> [!WARNING]
> **You can be banned from RIVALS for using skin changers.** Swapping weapon or character skins with Patchwork counts as one, even though only you can see it. Use Patchwork at your own risk, and try it on an alt account first.

## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v2.0.13-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 2.0.12.

## New

- **.obj models can replace meshes.** Patchwork converts them to Roblox's mesh format when it writes them (normals are filled in if the model has none; UVs are kept). Use them from **Cache → a mesh → Replace with file…**, or from a Fleasion preset's local `.obj` rules, which are no longer skipped on import.
- Empty or completely flat models are refused, so a swap still can't make a 3D object disappear.

## Swapping a weapon skin with .obj files (e.g. Frostbite Bow)

1. Close Roblox completely, open RIVALS and wait in the lobby.
2. **Cache → Watch**, then equip the weapon in RIVALS so it's drawn in 3D. **Refresh**: its meshes show under **Since watch**.
3. Click the weapon's main mesh → **Replace with file…** → pick the pack's body `.obj`. Do the same for the arrow (or other part) with its `.obj`, and for textures with the pack's images.
4. Close Roblox completely and open RIVALS again.
