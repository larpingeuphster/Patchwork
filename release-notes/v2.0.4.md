Clear Cache keeps your swaps, so they're there instantly afterwards.

> [!WARNING]
> **You can be banned from RIVALS for using skin changers.** Swapping weapon or character skins with Patchwork counts as one, even though only you can see it. Use Patchwork at your own risk, and try it on an alt account first.

## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v2.0.4-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 2.0.3.

## Changed

- **Clear Cache keeps your swapped assets.** It deletes everything else Roblox has saved, but leaves the entries holding your swaps, so Roblox finds them again straight away: no re-download and no waiting a match for swaps to come back.
- **Clear Cache works with Roblox open.** Patchwork no longer closes Roblox to clear the cache. Any file Roblox has open at that moment is left and cleared next time.
- Activity says how many swaps were kept, e.g. "Cleared Roblox's cache (1520 item(s)) and kept your 5 swapped asset(s), so they load straight away."

## Still true

An asset you've **never seen in game** has to be downloaded by Roblox once before Patchwork can swap it. After that it stays swapped through clears, restarts and new sessions.
