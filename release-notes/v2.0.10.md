## Download

Download **`Patchwork-v2.0.10-Windows.exe`** below. It replaces 2.0.9.

## Fixed

- **Models and sounds have icons** in the Cache tab instead of plain text: a speaker for sounds, a box for models and animations. Files whose preview can't be made (or hasn't loaded yet) get an icon for their kind too.
- Opening a sound shows its icon above the player.
