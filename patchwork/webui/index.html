RIVALS only

Swaps apply only while you're in RIVALS (place ). Patchwork reads Roblox's own log to see which game you joined. In any other game, Roblox loads its normal assets.

Staying safe

Patchwork never touches the game's memory or your account.

Certificate

Patchwork made a certificate just for this PC and added it to Roblox's own list (not Windows'). Removing it stops Patchwork and takes it out of every Roblox install.
