RIVALS only

Swaps apply only while you're in RIVALS (place ). Patchwork reads Roblox's own log to see which game you joined. In any other game, Roblox loads its normal assets.

Staying safe

Patchwork never touches the game's memory or your account. It starts swapping as soon as it opens; to stop it, quit Patchwork from its tray icon.

How swaps work

Roblox saves every asset it downloads in a cache on your PC. Patchwork finds the cached copy of each asset you swap and replaces it, so Roblox shows your version the next time it loads that asset (usually your next match). It never touches Roblox's connection, the game's memory or your account, and it works the same with Bloxstrap, Fishstrap or Froststrap.

Images, sounds and skins (a mesh or model swapped for another skin's) can be swapped. 3D objects can't be removed or made invisible.
